LocoMusa is a small, curated benchmark for local LLMs that measures **thinking-partner quality**: ideation, reframing, steelmanning, and multi-turn dialogue. It complements [LocoBanco](https://locobanco.org) (deterministic knowledge and reasoning) by measuring how well a local model works with you.
