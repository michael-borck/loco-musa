LocoMusa

Not "how smart is the model?" but "how good is it to think with?" A small, curated benchmark for local LLMs scoring ideation, reframing, steelmanning, and multi-turn dialogue — by blind pairwise judging against frontier-model anchors.

  • Repository on GitHub Tasks, harness, judge, and the full methodology
  • Leaderboard (JSON) Bradley–Terry ratings normalised so frontier anchors average 100
  • Methodology Why pairwise, why position-swapped, how humans validate the judge
Part of the LocoLabo family — the soft-metrics twin of LocoBanco. Unrelated to Salesforce's LoCoBench.
